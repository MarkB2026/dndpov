Faithful to the original Ravenloft I6 maps

DNDPOV recreates the original adventure’s geography and mapped dungeon locations as a walkable first-person companion, with artistic adaptations for 3D exploration. All 12 castle levels have been mapped in the full project; this restricted browser beta includes 2 complete levels. Explore with location descriptions, map guidance and encounter prompts. It does not resolve battles or replace tabletop combat rules.

What is DNDPOV? · Controls & beta information

DNDPOV (Dungeons & Dragons Point of View) is a stylized, first-person 3D companion map—not a standalone video game. The public beta is intended for desktop browsers. The map provides mapped locations, room descriptions and encounter guidance, but does not run or resolve battles. The environments include atmospheric effects such as changing weather, fog, day/night settings and ambient audio. Use the on-screen controls and instructions within each adventure area. Browser multiplayer is not part of this beta; the downloadable multiplayer edition is being prepared separately.
