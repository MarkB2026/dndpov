What the test tells us

Verbosity — working in this test.

The answers become noticeably more detailed. The slider appears to influence response length and narrative richness.

Heroism — still unproven.

The earlier bat encounter suggests the DM may remain too forgiving even with Heroism set to zero.

Adventure accuracy — needs improvement.

The DM introduced or embellished details about an inn, a contact, and the Burgomaster's summons. Those should be grounded in the adventure reference rather than added simply to make a response longer.

The next improvement

Keep the working Verbosity control. Focus the next Svalich update on making Heroism affect the DM's impartiality and consequences, while keeping every response grounded in the module.

Design principle

More words should mean richer narration of established facts — not invented adventure details.
