How DNDPOV works

Your adventure. Your rules. Your point of view. DNDPOV is a first-person exploration companion—not a video game or a replacement for the original adventure module.

What DNDPOV handles

  • Walkable 3D environments, ambient sound, and interactive maps with visited-area tracking.
  • Location-based boxed descriptions and optional DM-only instructions.
  • Automatic location-based, conditional, and random encounter checks where implemented.
  • Surprise announcements identifying creatures or events and prompting players to roll initiative when appropriate.

What stays at the table

  • Character choices, conversations, roleplaying, and storytelling.
  • Combat, initiative order, hit points, attacks, saving throws, and damage.
  • Your own dice, characters, rules, and Dungeon Master.
  • Your original adventure materials; DNDPOV is a companion, not a substitute for the module.

Choose your experience

Text ON/OFF shows or hides room descriptions and inline DM notes. Encounters ON/OFF enables automatic encounter checks and surprise alerts. DM ON/OFF reveals or hides DM-only instructions and encounter summaries. Visited Map ON/OFF shows or hides explored-area highlights.

No virtual tabletop campaign to configure and no platform-specific edition to purchase just to use the walkthrough. Launch in your browser and explore. You can also use a local copy with the required assets. Speechify narration is optional and controlled separately through Speechify.

How to play

Use DND POV on your own or bring it to your next group session.

Solo adventurers

  1. Use ChatGPT as your Dungeon Master and upload the adventure module to your chat.
  2. Use Speechify to listen to the narration if you prefer an audio experience.
  3. Open DND POV and walk through the environment as the story unfolds.

Videos are loaded from the /videos folder.

Recommended solo setup: ChatGPT Dungeon Master + uploaded module + Speechify + DND POV.

Group adventures

Explore together while your Dungeon Master runs the session. DNDPOV can automatically check and announce encounters, but your group still controls decisions, roleplaying, dice rolls, and combat.

Your group keeps control of the story, decisions, encounters, and dice rolls.
