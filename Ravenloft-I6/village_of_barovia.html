Village Crossroads

Tall shapes loom out of the dense fog that surrounds everything. The muddy ground underfoot gives way to slick, wet cobblestones. The tall shapes become recognizable as the dwellings of the village of Barovia. The windows of each house stare out from pools of black nothingness. No sound cuts the silence except for a single mournful sobbing mat echoes through the streets from a distance.

Walk toward the marked buildings. Press E at a door to enter; press E again to leave.

Village of Barovia

Explore a stylized, fog-covered village in first person—interconnected cobblestone streets, crooked timber houses, the church, tavern, mercantile, graveyard, and the burgomaster's home.

This is a walkable layout prototype, not a finished reconstruction of the printed village map. The keyed E1–E7 read-aloud and DM text is drawn from I6; street layout and architecture are interpretive.
